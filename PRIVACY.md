# LobbyFX Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2 October 2026_

LobbyFX is a browser extension that customises the look and sounds of faceit.com. It does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal data.

- **What stays on your device.** Your settings, background library, uploaded images, videos and sounds are saved only in your browser (chrome.storage). We have no server and cannot see them.
- **Network requests.** The extension loads only what you ask for: media files and pages you add by link (YouTube, Tenor, Giphy, myinstants or other sites you choose), the optional Workshop list from cdn.jsdelivr.net, and fonts from fonts.googleapis.com. Those services receive a normal web request from your browser and follow their own privacy policies.
- **Page access.** The extension runs only on faceit.com. It reads the page layout locally to apply themes and to find the Accept button for auto-accept. Nothing from the page is sent anywhere.
- **Idle state.** If you set an activity limit for auto-accept, the extension asks Chrome whether you have been active recently. This is checked locally and never stored or sent.
- **Removal.** Uninstalling the extension, or Settings → Reset in LobbyFX, deletes all of its data.
- **Children.** LobbyFX is not directed at children under 13.

LobbyFX is not affiliated with FACEIT or YouTube.
